Two Artists.

Sanjeeda Khanam and Sanjay Sahani are artists driven by a shared passion for creativity and visual expression.

Their work explores different emotions, ideas, cultural influences and perspectives through paintings and artistic compositions.

Every artwork is created with the intention of making the viewer pause, observe and experience something meaningful.

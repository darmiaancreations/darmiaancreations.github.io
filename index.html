THE JOURNEY

About the Artists

Dr. Sanjeeda Khanam and Dr. Sanjay Kumar Sahani are contemporary Indian artists whose artistic practices explore colour, nature, culture, human experience and the visual richness of everyday life.


Their creative journeys encompass painting and mixed-media approaches, bringing together observation, imagination, memory and personal experience. Through their works, they continue to explore different possibilities of colour, form, texture and contemporary expression.

THE ARTISTS

Meet the Artists

Discover the individual artistic journeys and creative languages of Sanjeeda Khanam and Sanjay Sahani.

ARTIST • PAINTER

Dr. Sanjeeda Khanam

Sanjeeda Khanam's artistic practice engages with colour, nature, movement and experiences gathered through life and travel. Her works explore acrylic and mixed-media approaches, creating compositions that combine atmosphere, visual rhythm and expressive colour.

ARTIST • PAINTER

Dr. Sanjay Kumar Sahani

Sanjay Sahani works across painting and mixed-media, exploring nature, landscape, human figures and cultural experiences. His work reflects an ongoing interest in colour, texture, observation and contemporary visual expression.
